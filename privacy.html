Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

RE ArtStudio ("the Studio") respects your privacy. This policy explains how our website and internal tools handle information.

2. Information We Collect

This website does not collect personal data from visitors. Our internal TikTok upload tool is used only by the studio owner and connects only to the Studio's own TikTok account. It accesses basic profile information (user.info.basic) to identify that account and uses the video.upload permission to upload the Studio's own videos as drafts.

3. Storage of Data

Access tokens are stored locally on the studio owner's computer. We do not store, sell, or share any user data with third parties, and we do not collect data from other TikTok users.

4. Third-Party Services

The tool uses TikTok's official APIs. TikTok's own Privacy Policy applies to data handled by TikTok.

5. Your Rights

You can revoke the tool's access at any time from your TikTok account settings under Security and permissions.
